Signal lime is never text or an icon on a light background — 1.16:1. It is a fill, and ink sits on top at 13.85:1.

Fairway ramp · single-series charts

"Shots lost by area" is one measure across four categories — one hue, focus item darkened. Four colours there would imply four unrelated things.
